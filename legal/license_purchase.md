# How to Purchase a Commercial License

Thank you for your interest in obtaining a commercial license for Aura Chatting Pro. Below is the normal process and information we need to evaluate and provide a license.

1) Contact

Email: redpixel.games@gmail.com
Please NOTE: Do NOT attempt to pay before contacting us — all commercial licensing requests must begin with an email inquiry. After initial contact we will provide payment instructions and invoice.

In your initial email, include:
- Company / Individual name
- Intended commercial use (short description)
- Estimated scale (number of users, instances, expected revenue model)
- Whether you will redistribute or sublicense the Software
- Desired timeline

2) Initial Evaluation

We will usually reply within 3 business days requesting any clarifying information. Based on your use case we will provide a proposed commercial license scope and an indicative fee (standard base fee USD 2,000, negotiable).

3) Payment methods (available after contact)

After successful negotiation and upon receipt of a written license agreement or invoice, we accept the following payment methods (details and instructions provided privately after initial contact):

- Cryptocurrency (BTC, ETH, or other agreed tokens) — payment details provided privately via email or secure channel.
- PayPal (business) — payment instructions provided after contact.
- Stripe (card) — we can provide a Stripe payment link or invoice after contact.

No commercial use may occur prior to execution of a written commercial license and receipt of any agreed fees.

4) Proposal & Agreement

We will produce a commercial license agreement draft (based on `legal/commercial-license-template.md`) specifying scope, fees, payment schedule, support terms (optional), and governing law.

5) Delivery

Upon receipt of payment (or after the agreed payment milestone), Licensor will deliver any required commercial artifacts (e.g., signed license, enterprise packaging, support onboarding credentials).

6) Contact & Escalation

If you do not receive a reply within 5 business days, resend or follow up to redpixel.games@gmail.com.

Notes
- No commercial use may occur prior to execution of a written license and receipt of agreed fees.
- The Licensor reserves the right to decline a license request for any lawful reason.
