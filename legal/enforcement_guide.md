# Enforcement & Detection Guide

This document outlines practical steps to detect and respond to unauthorized commercial use of the Software.

1) Proactive detection
- Monitor public code search engines (GitHub advanced search, Sourcegraph) for occurrences of the repository name or unique strings from the codebase.
- Set up Google Alerts for key project names and unique identifiers.
- Use GitHub search queries: `"Aura Chatting Pro" in:file` or search for unique function names or comments.
- Use marketplace and app store monitoring for product names or screenshots.

2) Evidence collection
- Take screenshots, collect URLs, and use `git blame` or archive copies to record the infringing content.
- Save timestamps and any accessible logs that show distribution or commercialization (e.g., product pages, pricing, ads).

3) Initial outreach
- Send a friendly inquiry email to the contact provided on the product listing asking whether they have a commercial license and provide a deadline to respond (typical 7–14 days).

4) Cease & Desist
- If no satisfactory response, send a formal cease-and-desist letter (template below) and demand either license purchase or cessation.

5) DMCA & platform takedowns
- For code hosted on third-party platforms, prepare DMCA takedown notices where applicable (note: DMCA applies primarily to U.S. law and platforms that honor DMCA).

6) Litigation & settlements
- If negotiation fails, escalate to legal counsel for potential injunctive relief or monetary claims. Provide your counsel with the documented evidence.

Sample Cease & Desist (short)

[Date]

[Infringer Name]
[Address]

Re: Unauthorized Commercial Use of Aura Chatting Pro

Dear [Infringer],

We represent the copyright holder of Aura Chatting Pro. It has come to our attention that you are using the Software for commercial purposes without a valid commercial license. You are required to immediately cease all commercial use and either (a) obtain a commercial license within 14 days, or (b) cease distribution and remove the Software from any commercial product. Failure to comply may result in legal action, including claims for damages and injunctive relief.

Sincerely,
RedPixel Games
redpixel.games@gmail.com

DMCA takedown checklist
- Identify copyrighted files and URLs
- Prepare signed DMCA notice
- Submit via platform's DMCA form and retain submission records
